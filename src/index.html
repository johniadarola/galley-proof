Manuscript

Type * * * on its own line for a scene break. In pasted or imported text, blank lines separate paragraphs, a line starting with # starts a chapter, and *words* become italic.

    History

    A copy is saved every 10 minutes while you write, when you open or close a book, and when you press Ctrl/⌘ S. Restoring saves the current text first, so nothing is lost.
